## Design Context

### Users

CSE undergraduates using the LMS to find coursework, revisit lectures, check attendance and timetables, and manage student records.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, considered. The user explicitly wants a very clean, premium interface and consistency across every page and element.

### Aesthetic Direction

The user selected the **Academic overview** concept from the supplied modern UI references. Use a cool neutral canvas, soft opaque surfaces with 24px corners, near-black actions in light mode and light actions in dark mode, a subtle static header tint, and legible Manrope typography. Use restrained sage for attendance and success states.

Navigation belongs in labelled pills at the **top right**, with no left navigation sidebar. The dashboard has a **calendar followed by the selected day's schedule on the left**, and academic overview/coursework on the right. Course names identify subjects; **do not display institutional subject codes** in cards or page headers. Internal route/data identifiers remain stable.

Both light and dark modes remain supported. Avoid glossy gradients, decorative avatars, social feeds, empty promotional heroes, and product jargon.

### Design Principles

1. Keep the calendar and day schedule together on the left of the desktop dashboard. Give other pages the full workspace; stack content comfortably on phones.
2. Use the same heading, button, surface, status, and spacing rules everywhere.
3. Show truthful states. Never claim a download, payment, submission, or sync occurred without an implementation.
4. Derive attendance from class counts and explain the per-course 75% requirement.
5. Make every page usable on a phone and with a keyboard; reduce motion when requested.
